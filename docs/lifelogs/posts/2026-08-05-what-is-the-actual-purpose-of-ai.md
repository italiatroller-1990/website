Nowadays, modern developers, students, and THAT certain type of person (AI addicts) use AI to either assist, or generate the stuff that needs to be done quickly. I think that's not it.

In my opinion, AI is supposed to be useful, not become a replacement, being a replacement sucks more than working side-by-side with 2 people, even if they hate you, y'all will do a good job.

AI should also be provide people with some knowledge that people didn't know existed, they fetch from other websites, like Wikipedia, ZDNET, The Verge, ...etc, and summarize them, making it easier for people to learn anything whom allergies are long texts.

AI can be also a test subject, test their limits, experiment with different topics, programming/scripting simple-but-powerful utilities, ...etc

AI shouldn't be commercialized for greed, it's supposed to make people open about technology and the development.

AI shouldn't be used to spy on people unlawfully, it's considered corruption!

AI shouldn't be used to replace humans, it's not good for literally everything programming related. There shouldn't be just humans, or LLMs, they need to work together.

AI shouldn't be used ***blindly***, it will cause more problems than solutions.

AI can't be perfect, and neither are us humans, and them other species.
