At first, using Automad feels awkward, but that's because you haven't learned enough. In this guide, I will show you the best features of Automad.

> **Wait!** This is for Automad v2, not v1!

## 1. In-page editing

In-page editing is a great quality of life feature on any CMSes. In Automad, when you're logged in, you can preview your pages and this will show up:

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785568186156.webp" alt="Automad toolbar" width="434" height="103" loading="lazy">

This is the ***toolbar***, hovering on these buttons will show you what will it do. To activate ***in-page editing***, press on the blue dial

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785568382674.webp" alt="Edit button" width="53" height="44" loading="lazy">

When activated, you will see these fields, press ***Edit*** to edit the elements of your page.

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785568428118.webp" alt="In-page editing view" width="1882" height="937" loading="lazy">

## 2. Components

Components are also a powerful feature of Automad. This can allow you to make custom blocks.

Creating a ***Component*** is easy, on the left sidebar of the dashboard interface, go to ***Components***.

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785568611104.webp" alt="Components sidebar" width="302" height="926" loading="lazy">

On the components page, this interface will show up:

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785568684891.webp" alt="Components page" width="1576" height="863" loading="lazy">

Press ***Add*** to add a component, then you can customize it however you want.

> **Note**
>
> Automad still has limitations, so make sure you customize your ***Components*** within it!

## 3. HTML/Markdown blocks

Other CMSes also provide you with HTML and Markdown blocks. But in Automad, HTML/Markdown blocks are more *powerful* than you think.

The ***HTML/Markdown*** blocks can have mixed syntaxes of both ***HTML and Markdown***, which is very useful for people who want to get a mix of code and regular text editing.

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785569238557.webp" alt="HTML/Markdown block" width="870" height="231" loading="lazy">

However, the block won't show anything on the editor, but will show on preview.

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785569027560.webp" alt="Editor view of HTML/Markdown block" width="868" height="329" loading="lazy">

<img src="/assets/images/guides/the-best-automad-features/image-1785569132373.webp" alt="Page view of HTML/Markdown block" width="601" height="118" loading="lazy">
