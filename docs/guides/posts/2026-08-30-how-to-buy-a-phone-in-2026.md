# Good starting points, because Y E S

For smartphones, you should aim for these common series choices:

- Samsung: A-series and S-series, may vary on price

- Xiaomi: mainline Xiaomi series, from 500$ - 1200$ (Used of few years ago - New)

- Google: Pixel a, mainline and Pro (or Pro XL if you're on 9 series or higher), may vary on price just like Samsung

- BBK Electronics - a "hub" for vivo, Oppo, realme and OnePlus: Not much choice here, but OnePlus from series 12 and up would do, starts from 300$ - 800$ (may vary)

- Motorola: a lot of choices here, price may vary!

If you want niche series of smartphones, look for:

- Fairphone, quite expensive, but you get repair-friendliness

- Nothing (owned by Carl Pei, not literal nothing), flashbang on the back (before 4 series)

- Pine64 and Purism, not really recommended, but if you want Linux preinstalled, get it!

However, if you wanted dumbphones:

- Nokia and HMD (modern series, non-smartphone)

- KaiOS dumbphones
